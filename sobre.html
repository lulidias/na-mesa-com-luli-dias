Nasci em Recife, Pernambuco, numa família portuguesa de terceira geração com raízes profundas no comércio — e na boa mesa. Cresci num ambiente em que a gastronomia faz parte do cotidiano: em casa sempre se respirou comida, vinho e hospitalidade.

I was born in Recife, Pernambuco, into a third-generation Brazilian family of Portuguese descent with deep roots in commerce — and at the table. I grew up in a world where gastronomy is part of everyday life: at home we have always lived and breathed food, wine and hospitality.

Je suis né à Recife, dans le Pernambouc, dans une famille brésilienne de troisième génération d’origine portugaise, aux racines profondes dans le commerce — et à table.
